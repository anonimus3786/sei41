The Largest School in the Region

School No. 41 opened on 2 September 2019 in the young Olshanka district of Grodno.

It is the largest school in Grodno region and one of the biggest in Belarus.

The building has 18,000 m², 84 classrooms, and 134 class groups.

Almost 4,000 students study here, taught by about 300 teachers.

📷 A. M. Kuznetsov
03 / 16

Why the Name Kuznetsov?

Aleksey M. Kuznetsov was a veteran of the Great Patriotic War who helped liberate Grodno in 1944.

He was an Honorary Citizen of Grodno.

In 2020, the pioneer group received his name. From 12 September 2022, the school itself was named after him.

His portrait greets students at the entrance and a museum exhibition is dedicated to him.

📷 School Rules
14 / 16

Our School Rules

To keep our school safe and friendly, every student must follow these simple rules:

1. Always wear a business-style uniform and be on time for lessons.

2. Put your mobile phone away in a special place before the lesson starts.

3. Treat teachers and classmates with respect and care for school property.

4. No bullying, smoking, or alcohol. We build a friendly community here!

5. Stay on the school grounds during lessons. You can only leave with a teacher's permission.

📷 A Typical School Day
15 / 16

A Typical School Day

My day starts at 8:00 with the first bell. We usually have six or seven lessons, each lasting 45 minutes, with breaks in between to grab a snack or just chat with friends.

The canteen has 360 seats, and there is even a special "vitamin zone".

After classes, many students stay for clubs, sports, or extra help. The school is busy from morning until evening because it also serves as a sports and cultural centre for the whole neighbourhood.
